Local knowledge

What we've learned installing in Ealing & West London

The side return is close to ideal

The side return on a 1930s semi is close to ideal for a condenser: out of sight, easy to service, and short pipe runs up to the bedrooms above.

Sizing for windows that stay shut

Under a flight path, cooling means you can keep the windows shut in July. We size for closed windows, which is a different calculation from the one most quotes are based on.

Chiswick's conservation areas

Chiswick has extensive conservation coverage around Bedford Park and the riverside - we treat those addresses the same way we treat Islington.
