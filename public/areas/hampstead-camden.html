Local knowledge

What we've learned installing in Hampstead & Camden

Camden's rules on external plant

Camden applies its planning policy on external plant strictly, particularly in the Hampstead, Fitzjohns and Primrose Hill conservation areas. A multi-split with one condenser instead of four separate systems is often what makes an application viable.

Lightwells and extension roofs

Where a house has a lightwell or a flat roof over a rear extension, that is nearly always the right home for the outdoor unit - screened, set on anti-vibration mounts, and positioned away from a neighbouring boundary.

Listed buildings and your architect

For listed buildings we work with your architect or heritage consultant from the start rather than turning up with a fixed idea.

Before you commit

We settle the outdoor unit first

It decides what you can have, what it costs and whether you need permission. We work it out at the free survey and tell you straight — including when the answer is that an application is unlikely to succeed.
