The model is deliberately pessimistic. Of the enquiries you never properly reply to, it assumes 40% come back to you anyway or were never real, so only 60% of the work you would have won is actually lost. Of that, it assumes Foreman recovers half, converting at the same rate as enquiries you already answer — in practice a fast reply converts better than a slow one, not the same. If the number still looks large, it is because the leak usually is. Nothing you type here is sent anywhere.
