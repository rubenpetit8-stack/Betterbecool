What we mean by qualified

Every lead clears all of this before it reaches you. If it does not clear, it is not sent and you are not charged.

  • The postcode is inside a patch you have told us you cover
  • They have said which rooms they want done, and roughly how big

  • Property type and tenure are known: house or flat, owner or tenant, freeholder consent likely or not

  • It is not a duplicate of an enquiry we have already sent you

Joining

How joining works

We check every installer before they see a lead, and we re-check every year. It is the only reason a homeowner trusts what we send them — which is the only reason the leads are worth anything to you.

  1. Apply

    Tell us the postcodes you cover, the work you want, and how many leads a month you can genuinely handle. Five minutes.

  2. We check you out

    F-Gas company certificate, REFCOM or equivalent, public liability cover, Companies House status and two recent customer references. We will not list you without all of it.

  3. Set your patch

    Choose boroughs and postcodes, set a monthly cap so you are never sent more than you can quote, and tell us what you do not want — no commercial, no flats, whatever it is.

  4. Leads start

    Usually within a fortnight of approval. Each one arrives by email and SMS with the full brief, and you confirm whether it was any good.

Installer questions

What installers ask us

The sceptical questions, answered properly. If yours is not here, ring and ask.

How is this different from Checkatrade or Rated People?

Two things. We qualify before we send, rather than passing on anything that arrives, so you are not paying to find out a lead was a tyre-kicker. And we cap how many installers get each enquiry at three, or at one on a territory plan — you are not racing five other vans to the phone.

What happens if a lead is rubbish?

Tell us within 72 hours and it is credited. Wrong or dead number, outside your area, already had the work done, duplicate, or obviously not a genuine enquiry. We would rather credit it than have you stop trusting what we send. Persistent credits on one plan usually mean we have your area or your filters wrong, and we will fix that rather than argue.

Do you take a cut of the job?

No. Never. You pay for the lead and the job is yours — the price is the same whether you win a single bedroom unit or a whole-house multi-split.

How many leads will I actually get?

We will not pretend to know before we have run your area. That is exactly why the first three are free and why pay-as-you-go exists: you find out what the volume and quality really are without committing to a monthly fee. Anyone who quotes you a confident monthly number for a patch they have not tested is guessing.

Who owns the customer?

You do, from the moment you make contact. We do not contact them again about that job, we do not follow up behind you, and we do not sell them anything else.

Am I tied in?

No contract and no notice period. Stop at the end of any month. A territory is the one exception — because it locks other installers out of a patch, we ask for a month's notice so we can reopen it.

What do you need from me to join?

Your F-Gas company certificate, REFCOM or equivalent registration, proof of public liability cover, your Companies House number and two recent customer references. We re-check the certificates every year and suspend anyone whose cover has lapsed.
