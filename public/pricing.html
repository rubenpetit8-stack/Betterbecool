What every installation price covers

If a cheaper quote doesn't list these, it isn't cheaper.

  • Free site survey and a fixed written quote before anything is booked
  • Heat-load calculation so the unit is sized to the room, not guessed
  • Supply of the indoor and outdoor units from a manufacturer-approved range
  • Up to 5 metres of insulated refrigerant pipework, drain and interconnecting cable
  • Core drilling, wall brackets and anti-vibration mounts for the outdoor unit
  • Connection to an existing suitable circuit, pressure test, vacuum and commissioning
  • F-Gas records, commissioning certificate and a walkthrough of the controls
  • Dust sheets, floor protection and all waste taken away
  • Manufacturer warranty registered in your name, plus our 2-year workmanship guarantee
