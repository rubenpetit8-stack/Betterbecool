Plain English

Your data, and your customers' data

You would be trusting us with your price book and your customer list — the two most commercially sensitive things you own. So here is exactly what happens to them.

We never train on your data

Your customer records, your conversations and your prices are not used to train any model, ours or anyone else's. They are not shared with other customers and they are never sold. If that is not in the contract you are offered, do not sign it — with us or with anybody else.

Your customers' details

When Foreman talks to your customer on your behalf, that conversation and those contact details belong to you, exactly as they would if your office manager had taken the call. We process them to run the service for you and for nothing else. You remain the data controller; we are your processor.

What we hold about you

Your account details, your price book, your enquiry and quote history, and the usage data we need to keep the service running and bill you correctly.

If you leave

We export everything — customers, conversations, quotes, price book — in a format you can actually use, and delete our copy within 30 days. There is no exit fee and we will not hold your data hostage to keep you.

Sub-processors

We use third parties for hosting, messaging and the language models themselves. We will name them on request and tell you in advance if that list changes.

Getting in touch

Email hello@foreman.build for a copy of what we hold, a correction, or deletion. If we have not put something right you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Before launch: this page states commitments — no training on customer data, processor status, 30-day deletion, named sub-processors. Have a solicitor turn them into a DPA you can hand to a cautious buyer, because the good ones will ask for it.
