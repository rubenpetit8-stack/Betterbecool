Plain English

What we do with your details

Better Be Cool is an introducer, not an installer. We pass your name, contact details and what you have told us about the job to up to three vetted installers so they can quote. We do not carry out the work, we are not party to the contract you sign, and we never charge you.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address, postcode, the rooms and systems you are interested in, your property type and your timescale. We do not run trackers, we do not use advertising cookies, and the quote builder keeps your list in your own browser until you press send.

Who we share it with

A maximum of three vetted installers, chosen because they cover your postcode and do the kind of work you have asked about. On some of our plans a single installer covers a postcode exclusively, in which case only they receive it. Nobody else gets your details. We do not sell them, and we do not pass them to insurers, finance companies or anyone else.

What those installers may do with it

Contact you about this job and quote for it. That is all. Our agreement with every installer bars them from using your details for marketing, from adding you to a mailing list, and from passing them to anyone else. If an installer breaks that, tell us and we will remove them.

How long we keep it

Two years from your enquiry, so we can resolve any dispute about work that came through us, then we delete it.

Changing your mind

Email hello@betterbecool.co.uk and we will withdraw your enquiry, tell every installer who received it to delete your details, and confirm when it is done. You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold, or to correct it. You do not need a reason and there is no charge.

Complaints

Tell us first — we would rather fix it. If we have not put it right, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Before launch: have a solicitor check this page and put a written data-sharing agreement in place with each installer. The commitments above are the ones the site makes on your behalf.
